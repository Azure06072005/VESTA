=== F000 [passing]: Environment & schema bootstrap ===
  [PRO] Superior Analytical Performance: DuckDB is an in-process columnar OLAP database, executing analytical scans over millions of records 10-50x faster than traditional row-oriented SQLite or PostgreSQL engines.
